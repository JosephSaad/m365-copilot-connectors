Connector onboarding

The questions to answer before a new source is connected. Answers are kept in this browser only — nothing is sent anywhere. Export when you are done.

No one person can answer this. Every question carries the stakeholder who owns the answer — first chip listed is the owner. Filter by role to produce the subset for one conversation, and hover the i on any question to see what each answer implies, why it blocks if it does, and how it affects sizing.

4 · Change detection
